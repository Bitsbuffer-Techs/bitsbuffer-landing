## Post 1: why-work-waits-manual-approval-chains (Operations)

- Angle: approval delay is a queue problem, not a judgment problem. Measure waiting time separately from decision time; remove steps before automating.
- STAT: ~60% of knowledge-work day is "work about work" (Asana Anatomy of Work Index, 10,000+ workers). https://asana.com/resources/anatomy-of-work
- STAT: 21% of orgs redesigned workflows before layering tooling (McKinsey State of AI 2025). https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai
- Hedged: Workfront 2021, 60% report slow-approval delays (vendor survey, labeled directional).
- First-hand: approval chain design in our own HRMS product (no product name pitch).
- Interlinks: why-custom-software-still-wins, signs-youve-outgrown-packaged-erp.

## Post 2: why-ai-pilots-fail-business-operations (AI & Automation)

- Angle: 95% failure rate is an integration failure, not a model failure. SMEs redesign workflows faster than enterprises.
- STAT: 95% of GenAI pilots no measurable P&L return, $30-40B invested (MIT Project NANDA, The GenAI Divide, 2025). Honesty section includes the published criticism of the 95% figure (Marketing AI Institute).
- STAT: 88% adoption vs ~6% significant impact; workflow redesign strongest predictor (McKinsey 2025).
- First-hand: our own Monday 5pm auto-report + metadata pipeline automations.
- Interlinks: ethical-implications-of-ai-enterprise-accountability, why-work-waits-manual-approval-chains.

## Post 3: cost-of-running-hr-on-spreadsheets (HR Operations)

- Angle: spreadsheets fail at handoffs, versions, and memory, not math. Cost is invisible because corrections are absorbed into HR hours.
- STAT: $291 avg cost per payroll error; 1 in 5 payrolls has one; 15 corrections/pay period; 1 in 6 firms had legal/compliance issues (EY 2022, primary PDF). https://eyquest.com/files/Cost_and_Risks_Due_to_Payroll_Errors_2022_Final.pdf
- Table: EY error-category costs ($705 sick time, $539 W-4, $499 benefits deduction).
- First-hand: our own 21-50 staff month-end attendance-to-payroll joins, Pakistan context (EOBI, provincial labour codes).
- Interlinks: signs-youve-outgrown-packaged-erp, /services/erp. CTA -> /services/erp.
